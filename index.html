About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Chonnam National University. My research interests lie in robust mobile robot localization and mapping, 3D perception, long-term localization, and SLAM for autonomous robots operating in dynamic and changing environments.

I am particularly interested in enabling robots to maintain reliable pose estimation and spatial understanding under challenging conditions, including noisy sensor observations, environmental changes, and uncertainty in spatial perception.
